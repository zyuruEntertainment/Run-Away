## About the Game

**Run Away** is a simple first-person survival horror game where your only objective is to survive.

You find yourself being chased by a relentless enemy. There is no complicated mission or elaborate story to follow. Your goal is straightforward: keep running, avoid getting caught, and survive for as long as possible.

How long can you last before getting caught?

## Features

- **First-Person Gameplay** — Experience the game directly through the player's perspective.
- **Enemy Chase** — Stay alert and avoid the enemy chasing you.
- **Survival Duration** — Choose your preferred survival challenge.
- **Main Menu** — Navigate the game through its main menu.
- **Settings Menu** — Access the available game settings.
- **Game Over System** — Getting caught means your run is over.
